About Me

"Taking chances to change myself and the world."

As an active Young Adult Volunteer of ASEZ WAO since 2016, I am eager to partake in environmental and health awareness. These small efforts of volunteer work hopes to help in making the world better.

As a Filipino, I bring and share my culture with others with the curiosity to learn about theirs. I regard diversity as a tool for growth professionally and personally as an individual and as a part of community.
